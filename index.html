About me

I'm also a longtime fan of mtg, dnd, and story-driven or open-world games like LA Noire and Darkside Detective.
I appreciate good design in unexpected places too — like the engravings and small details on cars.
Always happy to meet new people who share these interests, so feel free to say hi!
